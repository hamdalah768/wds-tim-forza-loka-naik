## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the drive, ingenuity, and community spirit of Indonesian local micro, small, and medium enterprises (UMKM). Built for an empowering digital entrepreneurship studio and educational space, the visual identity balances institutional credibility with approachable warmth. 

The design direction merges **Modern Humanist Utility** with **Tactile Botanical Warmth**:
- **Clarity & Trust:** Deep emerald anchors actions and institutional guidance, avoiding cold fintech tropes in favor of natural stability.
- **Vibrant Optimism:** Zingy lime and sunlit persimmon accents highlight progress, achievements, and real-time business opportunities without visual clutter.
- **Empathetic Simplicity:** Every interface layer is optimized for high legibility under varied lighting conditions and on low-spec mobile hardware across diverse Indonesian regions.
- **Authentic Materiality:** Visual layouts frame authentic physical textures—weaving, natural packaging, ceramics, and local culinary crafts—retaining human craft within a sophisticated digital platform.

## Colors

The color architecture is built strictly on high-contrast natural light aesthetics designed to reduce cognitive strain for entrepreneurs managing their businesses day in and day out.

### Palette Roles
- **Primary Emerald (`#08765B`):** The definitive brand anchor. Used for high-priority calls to action, brand markers, navigation headers, and solid state active controls.
- **Lime Accent (`#D6F264`):** The spark of progress. Dedicated to status badges, achievement tags, interactive callouts, and key highlight banners. Always paired with deep evergreen text (`#123C32`) to satisfy AAA accessibility standards.
- **Orange Warmth (`#F27A45`):** Secondary warm accent signaling urgency, notifications, upcoming live workshops, and dynamic commerce alerts.
- **Deep Evergreen Neutral (`#123C32`):** Primary typographic color replacing harsh pure blacks with organic, readable contrast.
- **Muted Slate Green (`#53675E`):** Secondary typographic color for captions, metadata, supporting text, and breadcrumb trails.
- **Structural Lines (`#D5E2D9`):** Subdued borders and dividing rules that frame content without breaking spatial flow.
- **Surfaces:**
  - `Canvas`: `#FFFFFF` for foreground elevations, active cards, and modular inputs.
  - `Soft Background`: `#F5F8F4` for main application screen backdrops.
  - `Muted Surface`: `#EAF2EC` for auxiliary panels, sidebars, and segmented control containers.
- **Feedback & Validation (`#B42318`):** Unambiguous crimson for error alerts, invalid input indicators, and critical cancellations.

## Typography

Plus Jakarta Sans provides a contemporary, friendly geometric silhouette with humanist nuances that deliver stellar legibility across web and mobile viewports.

- **Display & Headlines:** Heavy weights (700 and 800) with slight negative tracking convey confidence, leadership, and structured momentum.
- **Body:** Standardized at 14px and 16px with open, relaxed line heights (1.5x–1.6x) to facilitate comfortable reading during extended business learning modules and administrative management.
- **Labels & Microcopy:** Clear 600 weight pairings ensure buttons, inputs, tags, and data tables stay legible even on lower-density mobile screens.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout system is founded on an 8pt base grid with a 12-column adaptive fluid framework:

- **Desktop (≥ 1024px):** 12-column layout with 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters and a maximum content container of 1240px centered with dynamic page margins.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8-column layout with 20px gutters and 24px outer margins. Multi-column studio preview modules stack into structured 2x2 or unified block displays.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with 16px (`1rem`) gutters and 16px screen edge margins. Form steps convert into sticky top-progress capsule bars, and split previews collapse vertically into swipeable card panels.

Content zones utilize clear asymmetrical white space to emphasize focus without causing visual detachment.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses tonal surface layering combined with soft, botanical-tinted ambient shadows. Pure black drop shadows are prohibited.

- **Level 0 (Base Canvas):** `#F5F8F4`. Grounding base for page content.
- **Level 1 (Card & Module Resting):** `#FFFFFF` surface accompanied by a subtle border (`1px solid #D5E2D9`) and an ambient glow: `0px 2px 8px -2px rgba(18, 60, 50, 0.04)`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Floating & Hover):** `#FFFFFF` surface with `0px 10px 24px -4px rgba(8, 118, 91, 0.08)`, creating an inviting, soft elevation when hovering over learning modules or business tools.
- **Level 3 (Overlays, Drawers & Modals):** `#FFFFFF` surface with `0px 20px 32px -8px rgba(18, 60, 50, 0.12)`.

## Shapes

The design system uses deliberate, friendly curvatures to make business operations approachable:

- **Full Capsule (Pill-Shaped):** Used across all interactive buttons, chip selectors, status badges, and search bars to reinforce an encouraging, tactile interface.
- **Card Surfaces:** Standardized with expansive 20px to 28px radii (`rounded-lg` to `rounded-xl`) creating distinct, organic framing for multimedia, studio modules, and metrics.
- **Form Controls:** Set to a balanced 12px radius to preserve structural precision and readable alignment across dense data entries.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Emerald background (`#08765B`), pure white text, full capsule radius (`9999px`), 12px 24px padding. Hover: `#065B46`. Active: `#054B3A`.
- **Accent Action:** Lime background (`#D6F264`), deep evergreen text (`#123C32`), capsule radius. Hover: `#C8E84E`.
- **Secondary / Outlined:** `#FFFFFF` background with `1.5px solid #D5E2D9` and deep text (`#123C32`). Hover: border color `#08765B`, background `#F5F8F4`.
- **Tertiary Warm:** Tangerine background (`#F27A45`), pure white text, capsule radius, for time-sensitive studio bookings and flash mentorship sessions.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Tags:** Capsule shape with 6px 12px padding. 
  - *Active / Success / Fresh:* Background `#D6F264`, text `#123C32`, typography `label-caps`.
  - *Informational / Category:* Background `#EAF2EC`, text `#08765B`, border `1px solid #D5E2D9`.
  - *Urgent / Workshop Live:* Background `#F27A45` with white text.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Text Inputs:** White canvas with `1.5px solid #D5E2D9`, 12px radius, 12px 16px padding. Active focus state transitions border to `#08765B` with an emerald ring halo (`0 0 0 3px rgba(8, 118, 91, 0.15)`).
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** 20px rounded squares (checkbox) or circles (radios) with `2px solid #D5E2D9`. Checked state transitions to `#08765B` fill with crisp white checkmark iconography.

### Cards & Split Previews
- **Modular Studio Cards:** Pure white background, 24px border radius, `1px solid #D5E2D9` border. Inner content is padded with 24px. Photography inside cards maintains a nested 16px corner radius.
- **Split Preview Panels:** Asymmetric two-column cards pairing structured business form inputs on the left with a live rendered storefront or product showcase preview on the right (backed with `#EAF2EC`).

### Breadcrumbs & Steppers
- **Breadcrumbs:** Slate green text (`#53675E`) separated by soft slash delimiters (`#D5E2D9`), concluding with active deep evergreen text (`#123C32`).
- **Multi-Step Form Indicators:** Horizontal capsule bar progression where completed steps display filled `#08765B`, active steps display `#D6F264`, and upcoming steps rest in `#D5E2D9`.
